# Ω-APT / Ω-GRG R2 MAX

Status: **GENERATED RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE — NOT VERIFIED PHYSICS**

## 1. Goal

Upgrade Ω-APT / Ω-GRG from a conceptual framework into a proof-oriented, falsification-oriented research system.

The system must separate:

- **ontology**: what is assumed to exist,
- **syntax**: how candidate theories are represented,
- **semantics**: what models satisfy them,
- **observables**: what can be measured,
- **evidence**: what has been tested,
- **verification status**: what is proved, simulated, measured, replicated, or falsified.

## 2. Candidate primitive compression

Current candidate sequence:

[
{Distinction, Relation, Transformation}
Rightarrow
{Constraint / Admissibility}
]

The second is a stronger compression candidate, not an established reduction.

The R2 task is to test whether:

1. distinction can be encoded as non-equivalence constraints,
2. relation can be encoded as compatibility constraints,
3. transformation can be encoded as history/path admissibility constraints.

If all three succeed without circular definitions, Constraint becomes a lower-level candidate primitive.

## 3. Four-layer architecture

### L0 — Primitive layer
Minimal concepts not yet derived.

### L1 — Structural layer

### L2 — Physical layer
Causality, time, geometry, dimension, dynamics, fields, particles, energy, mass, entropy.

### L3 — Experimental layer

Every derivation must record an explicit path:

[

]

and must fail if a target concept is imported earlier under another name.

## 4. Anti-smuggling invariant

For every concept (C), define a dependency set:

[
Dep(C)={C_1,ldots,C_n}
]

A derivation of (C) is invalid if:

[
C in TransitiveClosure(Dep(C))
]

unless the construction is explicitly a fixed-point definition with an existence/uniqueness argument.

For each axiom set (A), search not only for models, but for impossibility results:

[

eg P

High-value outputs include:

## 9. Axiom ablation

For each axiom (a_i):

[
A^{-i}=Asetminus{a_i}
]

Evaluate:

- what derivations disappear,
- what new countermodels become admissible,
- whether empirical coverage changes,
- whether (a_i) was redundant.

This produces **Axiom Credit** instead of rhetorical importance.

## 10. Formal milestones

### Milestone M0 — representation
Machine-readable theory schema.

### Milestone M1 — minimal theory
Maximum 3 primitives, 5–10 definitions.

### Milestone M2 — proofs
At least 2 derived propositions with explicit dependencies.

### Milestone M3 — independence
At least 1 countermodel establishing independence or redundancy of an axiom.

### Milestone M4 — obstruction
At least 1 no-go theorem or formal obstruction.

### Milestone M5 — empirical decision
At least 1 discriminating prediction or experimental decision rule.

### Milestone M6 — regeneration
At least 1 successful mutation of the axiom set driven by a measured or formal residual.
